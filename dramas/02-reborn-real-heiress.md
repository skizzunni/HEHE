### Part 1 — I Died on Her Birthday

**Scene 1 — Hospital corridor, winter night**
🎬 Image: Empty dim hospital corridor, snow falling outside a tall window, Shen Zhiyi (older, gaunt, pale, long straight black hair loose, thin hospital gown, small mole under left eye) slumped on a metal bench, phone glowing in her hand showing a birthday party photo.
🎥 Motion: Slow dolly toward her; the phone slips from her fingers and clatters to the floor; fluorescent light flickers.

NARRATOR: "I died on my birthday."
NARRATOR: "Nobody noticed. Because it was *her* birthday too."
NARRATOR: "On my phone, my mother was cutting a seven-tier cake. My father was giving a speech about 'our only treasure.'"
NARRATOR: "I called them eleven times. The twelfth time, the battery died. A few minutes later, so did I."
SFX/MUSIC: Distant party music leaking from the phone speaker. Heart monitor flatline far away.

**Scene 2 — Flashback: hospital surgery ward, day**
🎬 Image: Two hospital beds side by side; Shen Ruoxue (wavy chestnut hair, rosy makeup) weakly smiling while Lin Wanqing (sleek chignon, pearl earrings, plum dress) holds her hand; in the other bed Shen Zhiyi lies alone with a surgical bandage, staring at the ceiling.
🎥 Motion: Slow pan from the mother's hands clasping Ruoxue's, across the empty gap, to Zhiyi alone.

NARRATOR: "When I was eighteen, they found me in a mountain village. The real daughter of the Shen family — switched at birth."
NARRATOR: "When I was nineteen, the fake one got sick. And guess whose kidney was a perfect match?"
LIN WANQING (tender, to Ruoxue): "My baby. Mama's here. Mama's not going anywhere."
NARRATOR: "Two beds. Three metres apart. She never once walked those three metres."

**Scene 3 — Flashback: Shen mansion living room, night**
🎬 Image: Luxurious marble living room, Shen Guohua (grey temples, navy suit) holding up a green jade bracelet in fury, Aunt Liu (grey bob, maroon cardigan) pointing at Zhiyi's open canvas bag, Ruoxue weeping on the sofa, Zhiyi standing frozen.
🎥 Motion: Quick push-in on the bracelet, then whip to Zhiyi's shocked face.

AUNT LIU (shrill): "Sir! It was in Miss Zhiyi's bag! The family heirloom!"
SHEN GUOHUA (roaring): "A thief! We brought a thief into this house!"
SHEN ZHIYI (shaking): "Father, I didn't—"
SFX/MUSIC: A sharp slap. Silence.
NARRATOR: "I didn't. But nobody asked. In that house, Ruoxue's tears were evidence. Mine were just noise."

**Scene 4 — Flashback: fashion week runway, night**
🎬 Image: Glamorous runway under white spotlights, Shen Ruoxue in pink bowing to a standing ovation, holding up a sketch of a silver plum-blossom necklace; in the dark back row, Zhiyi clutching an identical sketchbook.
🎥 Motion: Camera cranes down from the applauding crowd to Zhiyi's trembling hands on the sketchbook.

NARRATOR: "Grandma taught me to carve plum blossoms from scrap silver when I was six. By twenty, I could design anything."
NARRATOR: "My designs. My sleepless nights. My name — stolen. She walked that runway in a crown I built."
NARRATOR: "And when I said so, they called me jealous. My own mother called me jealous."

**Scene 5 — Flashback: cemetery, snow, day**
🎬 Image: Lonely snowy cemetery, a small grey headstone reading 沈知意, a single figure — Lu Jingchen (black cashmere coat, swept-back hair, eyebrow scar, matte-black wheelchair) — holding a black umbrella, laying a silver plum-blossom pendant on the stone.
🎥 Motion: Slow arc around Lu; snow collects on the umbrella; his gloved hand rests on the headstone.

NARRATOR: "Only one person came to my funeral."
NARRATOR: "Lu Jingchen. The crippled heir the whole city feared. They said he'd ruined three companies just for looking at him wrong. I had spoken to him exactly once."
LU JINGCHEN (low, broken): "You were supposed to wait for me."
NARRATOR: "I never found out what he meant. Not in that life."

**Scene 6 — Village farmhouse bedroom, autumn morning**
🎬 Image: Simple wooden farmhouse room with paper window, morning sun, Shen Zhiyi (18, healthy, long straight black hair, white cotton shirt) gasping awake on a wooden bed, a paper wall calendar showing September 3rd.
🎥 Motion: Sharp rack focus from the calendar to her wide eyes; she presses a hand to her side where the scar should be.

NARRATOR: "Then I opened my eyes."
NARRATOR: "No scar. No snow. Roosters. The smell of Grandma's millet porridge. September 3rd. I was eighteen again."
GRANDMA ZHOU (off-screen, calling): "Yiyi! Come quick! There's a big black car coming up the mountain!"
NARRATOR: "I knew that car. I'd dreamed about it for four years."

**The car that ruined my life was here. And this time — I was ready.**

### Part 2 — My Terms

**Scene 7 — Village courtyard, morning**
🎬 Image: Dusty mountain courtyard with chickens and drying corn, a gleaming black luxury sedan, Lin Wanqing (plum dress, cream shawl, pearl earrings) stepping out with a handkerchief over her nose, Shen Ruoxue (pink chiffon dress, pearl clips) clinging to her arm.
🎥 Motion: Low angle as the sedan door opens; a chicken flaps past Lin Wanqing's heels and she flinches.

NARRATOR: "Last life, I ran out barefoot and cried. I thought: my real mother came for me. Finally, someone came for me."
LIN WANQING (stiff smile): "So… you're Zhiyi. You look… healthy."
NARRATOR: "She meant 'poor.' I could tell, because she was looking at my shoes."
NARRATOR: "This time, I didn't run. I finished my porridge first."

**Scene 8 — Village courtyard, morning**
🎬 Image: Shen Ruoxue stepping forward with glossy tear-filled doe eyes, hands clasped, Shen Zhiyi (white cotton shirt, wooden hairpin) standing calm beside Grandma Zhou (silver bun, indigo jacket, bamboo basket).
🎥 Motion: Slow push-in on Ruoxue as a single tear rolls down on cue.

SHEN RUOXUE (trembling): "Sister, I'm so sorry. I know I'm living the life that should have been yours. I'll never take anything of yours. It's just… Mama can't sleep without me."
NARRATOR: "Last life, I said, 'It's okay, we can share.' I shared until there was nothing left of me to share."
SHEN ZHIYI (shrugs): "Then keep her."
SFX/MUSIC: Record-scratch beat.
SHEN RUOXUE (blinking): "…What?"
SHEN ZHIYI: "Your mama. Keep her. Sounds like you two are very happy."

**Scene 9 — Village courtyard, morning**
🎬 Image: Zhiyi holding up three fingers, Lin Wanqing gaping, Grandma Zhou hiding a grin behind her basket.
🎥 Motion: Handheld whip-pan between Zhiyi's three fingers and the mother's outraged face.

SHEN ZHIYI (flat): "I'll come. Three conditions. One — Grandma comes with me. Two — nobody touches my sketchbook. Three — I don't sleep in the servants' wing."
LIN WANQING: "You're in no position to make demands—"
SHEN ZHIYI: "There's a reporter in town asking why the Shen family left their blood daughter in a pig village for eighteen years. He left his card. Shall I call him back?"
LIN WANQING (through teeth): "…Get in the car."
GRANDMA ZHOU (whispering): "Yiyi, when did you learn to talk like that?"
SHEN ZHIYI (whispering back): "In my previous life, Grandma."
GRANDMA ZHOU: "Ah. Was it a good life?"
SHEN ZHIYI: "Terrible."
GRANDMA ZHOU (nodding seriously): "Then this one, we bring the pickled radish."

**Scene 10 — Inside the sedan, moving, day**
🎬 Image: Back seat of a luxury car, mountain road outside, Ruoxue in pink staring sideways at Zhiyi with a flicker of cold suspicion, Zhiyi looking calmly out of the window.
🎥 Motion: Slow focus pull from Zhiyi's reflection in the window to Ruoxue's narrowed eyes behind her.

SHEN RUOXUE (soft, testing): "Sister… you're not how I imagined. You're not supposed to be like this."
SHEN ZHIYI: "How was I supposed to be?"
SHEN RUOXUE (catching herself, smiling): "Oh — just… shy. Village girls are usually shy."
NARRATOR: "Not *supposed* to be. Interesting choice of words. I filed it away."

**Scene 11 — Shen mansion, small guest room, night**
🎬 Image: Modest guest room in a grand mansion, Grandma Zhou asleep under a quilt, Zhiyi at a desk lit by a laptop, sketchbook open to a delicate silver ring shaped like a plum blossom.
🎥 Motion: Over-the-shoulder push-in as she types "ZHI" into a brand registration page.

NARRATOR: "Here's what I knew that nobody else did."
NARRATOR: "In three months, a Paris buyer named Madame Vivienne would come to Shanghai hunting for one Chinese designer to launch worldwide. Last life, she found my sketches — with Ruoxue's name on them."
NARRATOR: "So this life, I'd use a name nobody could steal. A name nobody in this house would ever connect to the village girl in the guest room."
NARRATOR: "Three letters. ZHI."

**Scene 12 — Same room, dawn**
🎬 Image: Phone screen close-up showing a social post of the silver plum ring by "ZHI" with 2.3 million views, Zhiyi's face lit blue, a faint smile.
🎥 Motion: Notification counter spins upward; camera pulls back to reveal the sunrise through the window.

NARRATOR: "By morning, two million people wanted to know who ZHI was. Three luxury houses had sent messages. One of them was Shen Group."
NARRATOR: "My father was trying to buy my work. Over breakfast. While I passed him the soy sauce."
NARRATOR: "But a name isn't armour. Last life taught me that. I needed someone the Shens were afraid of."

**So I made the most dangerous appointment in Shanghai — with the man they called the Crippled Devil.**

### Part 3 — The Crippled Devil

🎥 Motion: The wheelchair turns slowly to face camera; a nervous assistant backs out and shuts the door.

NARRATOR: "His assistant told me the last person who came uninvited left in an ambulance. I think he was joking. I think."
LU JINGCHEN (cold): "The Shens' stray. You have three minutes."
SHEN ZHIYI: "I need two."
LU JINGCHEN (one eyebrow rising): "Confident."
SHEN ZHIYI: "Efficient."

**Scene 14 — Lu Group office, day**
🎬 Image: Zhiyi sliding a folder across a black marble desk to Lu Jingchen, a photo of an older man (Lu's uncle) clipped to it.
🎥 Motion: Close-up on the folder sliding; Lu's fingers stop it; his eyes lift.

SHEN ZHIYI: "Your uncle is selling Lu Group's port bids to your rivals. At Friday's board meeting, he'll use the leak to vote you out."
LU JINGCHEN: "And you know this how?"
SHEN ZHIYI: "Same way I know you're not as broken as you pretend."
SHEN ZHIYI (glancing down): "There's fresh mud on your soles, Mr. Lu. Wheels don't need treads."
SFX/MUSIC: Silence. A clock ticks.
NARRATOR: "For a second, I thought he might have me thrown out of the window. Instead—"
LU JINGCHEN (a low laugh): "Two minutes. You were right."
NARRATOR: "His assistant told me later nobody had heard him laugh in three years."

**Scene 15 — Lu Group office, sunset**
🎬 Image: Golden sunset light, Lu Jingchen and Zhiyi facing each other across the desk, a contract between them, two pens.
🎥 Motion: Slow orbit around the desk as both pens touch paper at the same moment.

LU JINGCHEN: "What do you want?"
SHEN ZHIYI: "A stage. Your Lu Group autumn showcase. ZHI debuts there — under your protection."
LU JINGCHEN: "And what do I get?"
SHEN ZHIYI: "Your uncle, gift-wrapped. With a bow."
LU JINGCHEN: "One condition. You never lie to me."
SHEN ZHIYI: "Then don't ask me anything you don't want answered."
LU JINGCHEN: "I'll take that risk."
NARRATOR: "He looked at me like I was a puzzle he'd already solved once — and was enjoying solving again."

**Scene 16 — Shen mansion, Zhiyi's room, night**
🎬 Image: Dark guest room, Shen Ruoxue (pink silk pyjamas, pearl clips) crouched at the desk photographing Zhiyi's sketchbook with her phone, the page showing a silver plum-blossom necklace labelled "Snow Plum."
🎥 Motion: Ruoxue's phone flash pops; camera slowly tilts up to a tiny hidden camera lens in a bookshelf.

NARRATOR: "Two nights later, my door creaked at 2 a.m. I'd left it unlocked. On purpose."
NARRATOR: "Forty sketches in that book. She didn't even flip through them. She skipped thirty-nine."
NARRATOR: "She went straight to *Snow Plum* — the exact design that made her famous in my last life."

**Scene 17 — Ruoxue's bedroom, night**
🎬 Image: Pink princess bedroom, Shen Ruoxue alone at a vanity mirror, her sweet face twisting into a cold smirk, phone showing the stolen sketch.
🎥 Motion: Slow push into the mirror reflection as her smile turns cruel.

SHEN RUOXUE (whispering to her reflection): "Same as last time, Sister. You draw. I shine. You bleed. I live."
SHEN RUOXUE: "And this time… you'll die even earlier."
SFX/MUSIC: Low string drone.

**Scene 18 — Zhiyi's room, night, phone call**
🎬 Image: Split screen — left, Zhiyi watching the hidden-camera feed on her laptop in the dark; right, Lu Jingchen in his dim study, phone to ear, firelight on his scar.
🎥 Motion: Both sides push in slowly as they speak.

SHEN ZHIYI: "She's reborn too."
LU JINGCHEN (no hesitation): "Then she knows the future."
NARRATOR: "He didn't ask what 'reborn' meant. I should have noticed that."
SHEN ZHIYI: "She knows *my old* future. I'm writing a new one."
LU JINGCHEN (pause): "…And the sketch she stole?"

**I drew it for her. With a trap inside.**

### Part 4 — The Trap in the Necklace

**Scene 19 — Lu Group autumn showcase, runway hall, night**

## Why It Works

- **Instant sympathy, instant hook:** The cold open flashes the climax line, then the first spoken line — "I died on my birthday" — earns the click within 3 seconds; the past-life montage stacks injustice (kidney, framing, stolen designs) so every later payoff feels deserved.
- **Mirror-scene payoffs:** Key past-life moments (the car arrival, the jade bracelet, the runway) are re-staged shot-for-shot, then flipped — the audience gets the dopamine of seeing *exactly* where the old story breaks.
- **Double-rebirth escalation:** Making Ruoxue reborn too turns a one-sided revenge into a chess match — and Zhiyi out-thinking someone who *also* knows the future makes her feel genuinely clever, not lucky.
- **Hidden-identity reveal (ZHI):** The family begs the very person they threw into the snow — the most reliable face-slap engine in the genre, timed for the climax.
- **Cold-male-lead chemistry with a secret:** The "crippled" heir who's faking, the mud-on-the-shoes deduction, and the "you were the only one who came" line plant a mystery that pays off in the standing-up moment and re-opens in the sequel hook.
- **Six self-contained Shorts:** Every Part ends on a bold cliffhanger line, so each cuts cleanly into a 9:16 Short that funnels viewers to the full episode.
