## Why It Works

- **Instant humiliation → instant power.** The fountain-helmet scene and "you're a dog" line create a grudge in the first 90 seconds; the system binds by minute three, so viewers are hooked before the usual drop-off point.
- **A system with clear, testable rules.** "Give sincerely, get 100x" is easy to grasp, and the escalating tests (¥50 → ¥25 → ¥7,000 → one life) plus the "Insincere. Return: ¥0" gag make the audience feel smart and give the creator easy, repeatable payoff beats.
- **The hidden clause is a planted bomb.** The ¥50,000 transfer is shown in Scene 1 and Rule Two is revealed in Part 5, so the ¥5,000,000 reversal at the gala feels earned, not random — the ultimate face-slap is the villain's own lie.
- **Every attack backfires.** Getting him fired, the showroom mockery, the "con man" dossier and the gala ambush each blow up on the attacker — a setup → humiliation → reversal rhythm that fits 60-second Shorts.
- **Ice-queen slow burn with a prop through-line.** Gu Xiyao goes from cheque-throwing suspicion to secretly washing his jacket; the jacket (Scene 14 → 18 → 24 → 36) and the "And if I give you my heart?" line turn the system mechanic into the romantic payoff.
- **Sequel bait built in.** The mysterious Granny Liu and "Host No. 2" open a rival-system arc and give viewers a reason to subscribe.
