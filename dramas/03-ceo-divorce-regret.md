## Why It Works

- **Instant injustice hook:** anniversary dinner + secret pregnancy test + divorce papers in the first 60 seconds — the audience is furious and invested immediately, and the cold open promises the payoff.
- **Stacked identity reveals:** "nobody" wife → Wen heiress → legendary Dr. Q, each revealed at a Part break so every Short ends on a "wait, WHAT?" beat.
- **Planted recognition details pay off physically:** the scar under his fingertips and the hummed lullaby are set up in flashback (Part 2) and cashed in at the mask reveal (Part 4) — viewers feel clever for spotting them.
- **Face-slaps at escalating scale:** a private signing in ten seconds → the impostor flunking one question → a public press exposure with receipts → the Bai family's business collapse.
- **Full "crematorium" regret arc:** he doesn't just apologise — he discovers the medicine soup notebook, the unopened cards, and kneels in the rain; the heroine keeps control and makes him start from zero, which comment sections love.
- **Comic relief + a held secret:** the chaotic brothers ("Rain's free.") keep the tone fun, while the hidden pregnancy (ginger tea, nausea, hand on stomach) creates dramatic irony that drives the twins sequel.
