Read the price, not the pick

This board shows every side of every game with its price, the probability that price implies, and how that price band has actually performed across 4,090 real sides of 2,045 completed 2026 MLB games settled at closing DraftKings numbers.

The table above is the point. Every favourite band won less often than its own price implied — heavy favourites by nearly four points, the −200 to −160 band by almost five. Underdog bands land within a point of fair. Backing every favourite blind returned −5.6%; backing every underdog blind returned −1.3%. Neither is a winning strategy — that is the vig — but the favourite is consistently the more expensive way to be right.

The DOG tag is not a recommendation. It marks the side the market prices as less likely, which is also the side it prices closer to fair. On 2026-08-30, four underdogs won of thirteen priced games and blindly backing all of them lost four units. A band being less overpriced does not make a night profitable.

Nothing here is a lock. A 14-game slate at a 57% hit rate swings between six and ten winners on an ordinary day, and the worst-priced band on the board is exactly the one parlays are built from.
