Whose pick this is, and what it is worth

The I have column is my own model, not the bookmaker’s favourite. Each league runs the method that won its own held-out backtest — MLB from starter ERA and FIP, bullpen ERA and team run rates; basketball, hockey and football from margin-of-victory ratings; tennis from ranking points. Why shows the numbers that produced the call.

Held-out accuracy: MLB 57.5%, NBA 73.1%, WNBA 70.4%, NHL 58.0%, tennis 57.1%. All beat a coin flip. None has been shown to beat a closing price — over 655 MLB games the market called 57.3% against my 55.9%.

So the Market column is there as a check, not a co-pick. A DOG tag means I am taking a side the book prices as less likely — the interesting disagreements, and also the ones most likely to be my error rather than the book’s.

Nothing here is a lock. Yesterday four underdogs won of thirteen priced MLB games, and my own top pick of the day lost to a +151 dog.
